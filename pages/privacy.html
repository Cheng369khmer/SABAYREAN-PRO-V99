Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 3, 2026

Welcome to CHENG Cyber (referred to as "we," "our," or "the Platform"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, download our shared books, or purchase our online IT and programming courses.

By accessing or using our services, you consent to the data practices described in this policy in accordance with the applicable laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you register an account, download digital materials, or enroll in a course.

  • Personal Data: Account details such as your name, email address, username, and contact information.
  • Payment & Billing Data: When you purchase a course, we collect necessary data to process your payment via secure third-party payment gateways (e.g., ABA Bank, KHQR, or local mobile wallets). We do not store your full banking credentials on our servers.
  • Course Progress & Activity: Information regarding the courses you enroll in, source code files downloaded, quiz scores, and certificate completions.
  • Technical Data: Your IP address, browser type, operating system, and access times automatically collected via cookies when you navigate our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to deliver a secure, smooth, and efficient learning experience. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the CHENG Cyber platform.
  • Process your course purchases and deliver digital book downloads.
  • Track your learning progress, manage lab exercises, and issue course completion certificates.
  • Send you essential updates, system administration notifications, and technical support messages.
  • Send promotional emails about new tech courses, books, or special offers (you can opt-out at any time).
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and protect the security of our infrastructure.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Third-Party Service Providers: We share data with trusted service providers who help us run our platform (such as payment processors, cloud hosting services, and email delivery systems).
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, regulation, or legal process within the Kingdom of Cambodia (e.g., compliance with the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications or relevant digital commerce regulations).

4. Security of Your Data

We implement appropriate technical, physical, and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or cyber threats. However, please note that no electronic transmission over the internet or storage technology can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Your Privacy Rights

In alignment with general data protection principles and local e-commerce frameworks, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: The right to request copies of your personal data or ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Deletion: The right to request that we delete your account and personal data from our active systems.
  • Opt-Out: The right to unsubscribe from marketing emails by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of our emails.

6. Intellectual Property & Fair Use

  • Shared Books: The books and downloadable PDFs provided on this platform are for your personal, educational use only. Unauthorized distribution, copying, or reselling of these materials is strictly prohibited.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any major changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
